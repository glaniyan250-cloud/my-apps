Start here

  1. Add a company manually — plug in the numbers from its financial statements using the form below.
  2. Or fetch from SEC EDGAR — enter a ticker and the tool pulls its latest annual filing data. You still enter the share price by hand (EDGAR does not publish prices).
  3. Or learn the UI first — press “Load sample data” to see three clearly-labeled SAMPLE companies, then remove them.

How it works: Stage 1 filters decide who gets to play (S&P 500 member, enough years of history, complete data). Stage 2 scores and ranks the survivors against their own industry benchmark. Companies that fail a filter appear in the Excluded view with the reason stated. Nothing here is investment advice.

Companies

Manual entry

Enter all money figures in the same unit (for example, $ millions), except price (per share) and share counts (actual share counts). Market cap = price × shares outstanding, so keep the unit consistent with the money figures.

Fetch from SEC EDGAR (best effort)

Looks up the ticker, then pulls the latest annual (10-K) figures from EDGAR companyfacts. EDGAR does not publish share prices — enter the price by hand after fetching. If the fetch fails (network, SEC blocking direct browser requests, or an unknown ticker), the form is left for manual entry and nothing is presented as real data.

Benchmarks by sector (editable) — every score is measured against the company's own sector

Defaults are starting assumptions, not market data — edit them freely. Clear a sector's row to score its companies against the median of entered peers in that sector instead. If a sector has neither a benchmark nor peers, its metrics score a neutral 50 and the detail view says so.

Scoring weights (editable)

Weights are relative. Metrics with no data are left out of the total and the detail view says which ones.

How the scores work

FCF margin = (operating cash flow − maintenance capex) / revenue.
Owner earnings = net income + depreciation & amortisation − maintenance capex.
Owner-earnings yield = owner earnings / market cap (price × shares outstanding). Also shown against the 10-year Treasury rate you set.
ROE average and consistency come from the yearly ROE series when entered, otherwise from the single-year figure; consistency reflects how steady the series is.
ROIC proxy = owner earnings / (equity + debt). Debt / equity = total debt / equity.
Margin stability comes from the yearly FCF margin series when entered.
Share-count change = (now − 5 years ago) / 5 years ago; a shrinking count means buybacks, a growing count means dilution.
Each metric scores 0–100 against its sector benchmark (higher is better, except debt / equity and share-count growth, where lower is better). Verdicts: 70+ worthy of a deep dive, 50–69 watch, below 50 pass.
