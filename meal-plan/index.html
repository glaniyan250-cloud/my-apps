Flavour arsenal (near-zero calories)

Hot sauce, mustard, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, cumin, paprika, cinnamon, black pepper, lemon juice. These are what make the same five foods taste like twenty meals. The only things you measure are the fats: butter, salad cream, coleslaw, nuts.

Sunday prep — about an hour

  • Cook a big batch of rice; portion 1½-cup servings.
  • Portion salmon into 200g fillets; brown the ground turkey with salt, pepper, garlic.
  • Boil Thursday's turkey wings; portion coleslaw into 80g containers (it gets watery if it sits dressed — keep the salad cream separate and mix before eating).
  • Hard-boil a few extra eggs for emergency protein.
  • Refrigerate 3 days' worth; freeze the rest and pull the night before.

Strict-phase rules

Weigh it. Proteins weighed raw, rice weighed cooked. Eyeballing is how the last plan drifted.
Creatine: 5g daily, any time, with water. Timing doesn't matter; consistency does. It won't slow fat loss, but it can add 1–2 kg of water weight on the scale — so trust the tracker's trend and your tape measurements over the first couple of weeks before cutting calories further.
Wine: strict phase = skip. One glass is ~150 kcal with zero protein — if you have one, drop the nuts and halve the lunch rice that day.
Shawarma (occasional): make it dinner. Chicken shawarma plate, easy on the wrap/rice, load the salad. Skip the evening snack that day.
Water: 3–4 litres a day. Hunger on a cut is often thirst.
Salad cream and coleslaw are measured. They are the two foods here that can quietly double the day's fat. 80g coleslaw, no free pouring.

When to change it

Don't change the plan on a daily weigh-in. After 2–3 weeks, check the tracker's 7-day average: not falling 0.4–0.6 kg/week → drop 200 kcal (cut the rice to 1 cup at lunch). Energy or lifts collapsing → add 200 back. The plan serves the trend, not the other way around.
